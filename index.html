This year, all unloading and sorting happens outside along a line of tables in front of the pantry. Food goes inside only after it is sorted, boxed, and weighed. On Sunday, a smaller crew finishes sorting and shelving inside. Sign up for a shift, offer equipment, or both.

How the line works

Each role is one stop along the line. Scouts rotate, so pick any roles you would like to try.

  1. 🚗
    GreetDirect cars forward
  2. 🛍️
    UnloadTake food from cars
  3. 🛒
    HaulWagon food to tables by category
  4. 🥫
    SortSort into pantry subcategories
  5. 📦
    RunCarry full boxes to the scale
  6. ⚖️
    WeighPantry staff log totals
  7. 🗄️
    StockShelve food, return empty boxes
